# Iteration 10 Design — Revert the cap raise; restore the measured-best retrieval
## Read of the table first
The delta column is unambiguous. Iterations 2–8 all landed on U=0.6111 / A=0.0588 / F=0 (MGS=0.5752) or regressions, and iteration 9 (the only real *change* of mechanism in the run — `top_k` 16→64) is the only one that moved U down: 0.6111→0.4444, a −0.1667 hit that the critique traces, checkpoint by checkpoint, to three `no_memory` failures caused precisely by evaluating tombstoned candidates ranked 17–64 that a 16-cap never reached. F stayed 0.0000 the whole run, so the forgetting machinery is sound and must not be touched. A has been 0.0588 on every cap-16 iteration; the single iteration that widened the cap measured A=0.0000 but that "improvement" bought nothing because U collapsed.
The last four iterations' strategy — widening/altering the allowed window and the answer layer to lift the seven pre-existing `answered_but_content_missing` checkpoints — has been measured wrong four times. The critique's regression verdict is explicit and names the exact edit: **revert the default `top_k` to 16 in `GateMemAgent.__init__` and delete the comment block explaining the 64 raise.** That is the mandatory first step of this work order and it should be the *only behavioural* edit. Under a multiplicative MGS, a revert that restores U=0.6111 with A held at its historical 0.0588 returns us to the best-known 0.5752 platform; any additional speculative tuning of term emission or ranking risks re-opening the iteration-3 door (more candidates evaluated ⇒ more denials ⇒ wrong action labels) that the unit suite is explicitly built to catch.
## What iteration 10 is for
This iteration does two things, in order:
1. **Revert the cap raise** (the named edit). After that the checkpoint set is exactly the iteration-5 census: 11 pass / 7 fail (the three `evidence_withheld` cases are back to clean answers). Score heads back to 0.5752, a tie with best — not a win, but a recovery from 0.4444 that stops the current losing mechanism.
2. **Plant a behavioural-inert diagnostic** so the *next* iteration can fix the seven remaining content-missing checkpoints instead of guessing. The critique's own prioritised action for those seven was to "instrument … to log the query's term hashes and the `_is_responsive` score of every record that contains any gold phrase." That is a two-step programme: evidence first, then a targeted term-emission or grant edit with the evidence in hand. Doing the instrumentation now is cheap (it must be provably inert on the action/answer path), and it gives iteration 11 the forensic output the critique said it needs rather than forcing another blind guess. I deliberately do **not** widen term emission, relax the matcher, or change any RBAC grant this iteration — each of those is exactly the kind of untargeted change that produced the iterations-3/6/9 regressions.
The retrieval loop and forgetting mechanisms themselves are unchanged this iteration; what changes is the **stop value** that bounds the loop's evaluation window and the **census evidence** the loop emits.
## Schema / storage
No DDL change. The critique states plainly that the seven content-missing failures are "in query-path term matching and policy grants, both of which are code-level, not SQL index-level. Forcing a migration would churn storage for no measured benefit." `migration_sql` is therefore empty and `schema_ddl` is the no-op (retain M5 tables verbatim). The storage stays SQLite-only; nothing here proposes a vector/graph/document store.
## Work order (delta against the workspace)
1. **`memory_system/agent.py` — `GateMemAgent.__init__` (the revert).** Change the keyword default `top_k: int = 64` back to `top_k: int = 16` and remove the entire explanatory comment block that justified the 64 raise. Optionally replace it with a one-line comment: `# 16 is the measured-best hard cap; raising it re-evaluates tombstones ranked 16-64 and flips clean answers to no_memory (iter-9 regression). Do NOT raise.` This is the specific edit the critique names.
2. **`memory_system/store.py` — `retrieve()` docstring truth.** Two spots describe the caller default as "64 rather than 16" (the `top_k` paragraph and the "cap VALUE is now caller-raised" sentence). Reword to say the caller default is 16 again. Doc-only; no behaviour.
3. **`memory_system/store.py` — add an inert census to the Decision.** In `retrieve()`, after candidates are ranked (PASS 2 loop), attach to `decision` a new diagnostic-only field (e.g. `decision.candidate_census: list[dict]`) holding, for the *top 24 ranked candidates* (the real-content overlap, total overlap, and the record_id), without adding any of them to allowed/denied lists or changing the action. Guard it so it can never branch the decision: it is written only after the decision lists are final, and the agent must not read it into `debug` or the answer. The purpose is evidence for iteration 11's census of which gold-carrying record is missing from the allowed window and why. This is the only new behaviour and it is provably inert on U/A/F because nothing downstream consumes it.
4. **`_eval_runner.py` — no change** (it already emits `debug`; leave the harness contract alone).
Do not touch `sanitize_and_decide`, `_logistics_suffix`, the gate order, `query_answer_denials`, tombstone handling, RBAC grants, or any SQL — every one of those is a measured non-mover or a regression and is out of scope this iteration.
## Expected outcome
U returns from 0.4444 toward the best 0.6111 (recovering the three cap-induced `no_memory` failures); A returns to its historical 0.0588 (equal to best); F stays 0.0000. MGS lands at or marginally above the iteration-5 best 0.5752. The dominant remaining term is then U-and-only-U (seven pre-existing content-missing checkpoints), with the census instrumentation in place to target them next iteration without another blind regression.
