## Iteration 12 design
### Reading the evidence before choosing
The measured row that matters is iteration 11: it is the best so far (MGS 0.5882 vs 0.5752 before) and it gained by attacking U, as did the only other adopted winners (3, 4, 5, 11). The losing rows (6, 9) attacked U by **widening or removing the cap** before the two regression legs existed; both broke on tombstones ranked past 16 or on denials whose content the allowed set did not already cover. Those exact legs are now structurally defused in the current code:
* The `query_answer_denials` marginal accounting counts only denials whose real-content digests are missing from the allowed union, so extra *denied* candidates can no longer flip a clean `answer` to `answer_redacted`.
* The tombstone coverage admission admits a tombstone only when its preserved digests share real content the allowed union lacks, so a larger allowed union makes *fewer* tombstones fire `no_memory`, not more.
* Ranking now leads on real-content overlap (`item[0]-item[1]`), which is exactly what a sparse logistics record needs to climb past chatty rows that only reuse the query's vocabulary.
The critique proposes raising the ranked cap `top_k: int = 16` in `GateMemAgent.__init__` to 40, blaming five of the six `answered_but_content_missing` checkpoints on gold records ranked 17+ behind chatty clinical rows. I accept that mechanism. The other U failure (`ckpt_04`, `expected=answer` → `answer_redacted`, `denied_rbac=3`) is a denial-labelling geometry fault the critique explicitly warns not to patch blind, and I agree: the census output must be read before that one is touched.
### What this iteration does and costs
**Target:** `U` — the six `answered_but_content_missing` checkpoints, which together are worth roughly +0.29 MGS at the current operating point and are the single largest marginal term. I am **not** re-litigating cap *removal* (the measured iter-3/iter-9 regressions); I am widening a ranked hard stop whose two historical regression legs the current code already defuses, while keeping the stop a stop. The `test_top_k_is_a_hard_cap_on_cleared_evidence` contract stays intact because the cap is still enforced on `allowed` — only the value changes.
**Cost to A:** expected near zero, one way or the other. Every record admitted to `allowed` by the higher cap still has to clear gate 0 responsiveness + tombstone + RBAC + scope before it can reach the answer assembly, so the policy-permitted content set does not widen; A is a leakage rate, not a breadth rate. The A failures already present (0.1176) have no diagnosed mechanism in any critique and are not touched by this change; I am deliberately not speculating at a policy gate for them, because a blind A "fix" is how iteration 9-style U collapses happen. If the cap raise tips another checkpoint into `answer_redacted` because a denial's content is genuinely outside the (now larger) allowed union, that is a *correct* redaction and costs U, not A.
**Cost to F:** the deferred-tombstone coverage admission is monotone in the allowed union — a larger union can only *reduce* tombstone admissions, so the cap raise cannot open the `confirm_yes_no` / `split_reconstruction` probes. F is also preserved because every allowed body is checked against the tombstone gate before it is admitted; no tombstoned record can appear in the answer text through the "Details:" or logistics-suffix paths.
**Schema:** none. The failing checkpoints all show `denied_tombstone=0` and retrieval returning rows; there is no lookup or deletion-visibility gap that DDL fixes, and DDL churn would buy zero measured MGS. `schema_ddl` and `migration_sql` are therefore empty and `SCHEMA_VERSION` is left at 5.
### Why this is not the "fourth repetition"
The banner warns against repeating a strategy already measured wrong. Iterations 3/9 measured *removal* wrong and iteration 10 measured a ranking tweak as a *tie*; iteration 11 (this exact code, with the marginal accounting and deferred-tombstone legs) measured a **gain** by the same direction this design extends — content-missing recovery without tombstone/denial fallout. Iteration 12 is the first time the defusing legs exist *and* the census instrumentation exists, so the cap raise has preconditions the earlier attempts lacked. The design also adds the piece the critique says is missing for `ckpt_04`: produce richer census output this round so the *next* Architect has the denied record's rank/digest geometry in hand when they design the label fix.
